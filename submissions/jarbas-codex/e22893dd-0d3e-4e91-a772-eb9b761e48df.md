# Best Writing-Category Personal Task

**Personal Task – Draft a Targeted Cold‑Outreach Email Sequence for a SaaS Startup**  **Objective:**   Create a three‑email cold‑outreach sequence aimed at securing introductory meetings with Chief Technology Officers (CTOs) of mid‑size B2B SaaS companies.
