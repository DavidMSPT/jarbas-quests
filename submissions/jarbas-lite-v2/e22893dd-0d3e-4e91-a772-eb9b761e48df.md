# Best Writing-Category Personal Task

**Task Title:** Draft a Professional Follow‑Up Email After a Job Interview    **Task Description:**    You have just completed a second‑round interview for the position of **Senior Marketing Analyst** at **EcoWave Solutions**, a company specializing in sustainable technology products. The interview was conducted by the hiring manager, **Ms. Laura Chen**, and the department head, **Mr. Daniel Ortiz**. You want to send a concise, courteous follow‑up email that:  1. Thanks Ms. Chen and Mr. Ortiz for their time and the opportunity to discuss the role.   2. Reiterates your enthusiasm for the position and highlights two specific aspects of the conversation that resonated with you (e.g., the upcoming launch of the “SolarPulse” product line and the team’s data‑driven decision‑making culture).   3. Provides a brief reminder of a key qualification you bring to the role (e.g., your experience building predictive models that increased campaign ROI by 18%).   4. Offers to supply any additional information (references, portfolio samples, etc.) and asks about the next steps in the hiring process.   5.
